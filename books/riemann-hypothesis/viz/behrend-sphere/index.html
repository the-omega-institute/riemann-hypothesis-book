ARGUED中点为什么总在球里：平行四边形公式 ‖(a + b)/2‖² = (‖a‖² + ‖b‖²)/2 − ‖a − b‖²/4 = R − ‖a − b‖²/4 < R（a ≠ b）。贝伦德（1946）把最胖的球壳写成不进位的整数（每位数字小于进制的一半），在 {1, …, N} 里得到不含三项等差、至少 N·exp(−C√log N) 个元素的集合。文献结果，未在本书形式化。Why a midpoint is always inside: the parallelogram law ‖(a + b)/2‖² = (‖a‖² + ‖b‖²)/2 − ‖a − b‖²/4 = R − ‖a − b‖²/4 < R (a ≠ b). Behrend (1946) writes the fattest shell as integers whose digits stay below half the base, so that adding two never carries, and obtains a subset of {1, …, N} with no 3-term progression and at least N·exp(−C√log N) elements. Literature result, not formalized in this book.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

立方体 {0, …, M − 1}³ 的每个格点都有一个“半径平方” R = x² + y² + z²，球心在立方体的一个角（原点）。同一个 R 的点在同一层球壳上。页面画出一层壳与壳上的格点；读者点两个壳上的点 a、b，就能看见它们的中点陷进球里。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 依据的章节。第六章 · 为什么整数球壳永远躲得开三项等差数列。
  2. 球面往外鼓。a、b 在同一层壳上且 a ≠ b，则 ‖(a + b)/2‖² = R − ‖a − b‖²/4 < R：中点严格在球里，不可能在壳上。三项等差 a, c, b 要求 c = (a + b)/2，所以一层壳上没有三项等差（ARGUED；页面在 M = 3 … 6 的每一层上逐一核对，NUM）。又因为 ‖a − b‖² = 2R − 2a·b 是偶数，中点至少下沉 1/2。
  3. 鸽巢。M = 5 时 125 个点只落在 32 层非空的壳上，必有一层至少 ⌈125/32⌉ = 4 个点；实际最胖的是 R = 17，9 个点。维数升高时，最胖的一层仍占全部点的一个不太小的比例：这是贝伦德（1946）构造的出发点，他把这一层写成不进位的整数，得到 {1, …, N} 中不含三项等差、至少 N·exp(−C√log N) 个元素的集合（ARGUED，文献结果，未在本书形式化）。
  4. 它怎样接到证明里。按第六章的叙述，挑选岩泽塔的层级时要避开“等差共振”，否则相消误差会沿同一条轨道叠加。形式化里用的是更一般的“无小系数线性关系”的集合（Formalism/BehrendSets/Statement.lean 里的定义 BehrendFree：α + β + γ = 0、系数不超过 K 时 αx₁ + βx₂ + γx₃ = 0 只有平凡解；三项等差是 α = γ = 1、β = −2 的情形）。第六章从这一部分引用了 select 与 trivStab（LEAN ✓），整条链收束于 everyCharacter（LEAN ✓）。页面不演示这条链，只演示小立方体里经典的球壳把戏。
  5. 示意与简化。立方体的尺寸、墙与玻璃的颜色、相机角度是画面的选择（MODEL）；格点、半径平方、计数与中点都是精确的整数计算。

Every lattice point of the cube {0, …, M − 1}³ has a squared radius R = x² + y² + z², measured from a corner of the cube, the origin. Points with the same R lie on the same spherical shell. The page draws one shell and its lattice points; click two points a, b of the shell and you see their midpoint sink inside the ball. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The chapter behind it. Chapter 6 · why integer shells always avoid three-term progressions.
  2. A sphere bulges outward. If a and b lie on one shell and a ≠ b, then ‖(a + b)/2‖² = R − ‖a − b‖²/4 < R: the midpoint is strictly inside the ball and cannot be on the shell. A 3-term progression a, c, b needs c = (a + b)/2, so a shell holds none (ARGUED; the page checks every shell for M = 3 … 6 one by one, NUM). And since ‖a − b‖² = 2R − 2a·b is even, a midpoint sinks by at least 1/2.
  3. Pigeonholes. For M = 5 the 125 points lie on only 32 non-empty shells, so some shell holds at least ⌈125/32⌉ = 4 of them; the fattest, R = 17, holds 9. In higher dimensions the fattest shell still holds a not-too-small share of all the points: that is where Behrend's construction (1946) starts. He writes that shell as integers that never carry and obtains a subset of {1, …, N} with no 3-term progression and at least N·exp(−C√log N) elements (ARGUED: literature result, not formalized in this book).
  4. How it enters the proof. As Chapter 6 tells it, the levels of the Iwasawa tower must avoid "arithmetic resonance", or cancellation errors pile up along one orbit. The formalization works with a more general kind of set, one with no small linear relation (the definition BehrendFree in Formalism/BehrendSets/Statement.lean: when α + β + γ = 0 and the coefficients are at most K, αx₁ + βx₂ + γx₃ = 0 has only trivial solutions; a 3-term progression is the case α = γ = 1, β = −2). From that part Chapter 6 cites select and trivStab (LEAN ✓), and the chain ends in everyCharacter (LEAN ✓). The page does not show that chain; it shows only the classical shell trick in a small cube.
  5. Models and simplifications. The size of the cube, the colours of the walls and the glass and the camera angles are choices of the picture (MODEL); lattice points, squared radii, counts and midpoints are exact integer computations.
