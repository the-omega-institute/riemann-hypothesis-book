理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

一面镜子只照一遍；把 Theta 提升首尾串成一条长廊，每多一面镜子就多一层“导数”。问题是这条链能走多远、走到哪里会被成本吃光。本页把这笔账画成两个场景，共用一条时间轴：链长预算 b = m/√X。本页是数值演示与示意模型，不是证明。

  1. 镜链。舞台左边是 U(X₀) → U(X₁) → … → U(Xₘ)：每个节点是一次 Theta 提升，取 √X = 5，所以 m = 5b。琥珀色是正在进行的那一步。单特征时链是一根品红的线；胶合正交成立（g ≥ 0.6）时，是三根青色的线在每个节点上胶合在一起。
  2. 跷跷板。链的尽头连着一个紫色菱形：左边的提升周期身上挂着 m 个赫克特征 μ₁ … μₘ，跷跷板把它换成右边的贝塞尔周期，那里一个特征也不剩。积分所用的大胞腔坐标是 Lean 定理 bigCellMinors；把一面“镜子”倒回去只差一个标量 |𝔽|，是 final_gaussOp，读数在 𝔽₅ 上逐项验算。
  3. 2/3 墙（示意模型）。右边的曲线是零点自由边界 σ 随 b 的走向。品红曲线 σ₁ = 2/3 + ⅓e−b/0.45 从 σ = 1 出发，b = 0.2（m = 1）时约 0.88，然后贴着 2/3 墙走，永远不越过去。青色曲线 σg = σ₁ − e/6 在胶合正交成立时越过 2/3，贴向绿色的 σ = 1/2 线（第一章引用的 Lean 主定理正是零点都在这条线上；这里的曲线只是示意，不是那条证明）。曲线的形状是为画面选的，不出自任何 Lean 定理。
  4. HN 屋顶（精确的凸几何）。秩 7 的格 L 里一串饱和子格给出 (rk, deg) 点 (0, 0)、(1, h₁)、(3, 4.4)、(5, 5.4)、(7, 5.0)。页面用单调链算出它们的上凸包：h₁ = 1.8 时四段斜率 1.8 > 1.3 > 0.5 > −0.2，屋顶向上拱起；把 h₁ 拖到 4.4/3 ≈ 1.467 以下，点 (1, h₁) 掉到屋顶下面，凸包直接从 (0, 0) 跨到 (3, 4.4)。琥珀色是最陡的第一根椽，斜率就是 μmax；灰色虚线是平均斜率 μ(L) = 5/7 ≈ 0.714。
  5. 屋脊逼出短向量（Lean）。shortVector：L 里有非零向量不长于 √rk·covol1/rk = √7·e−5/7 ≈ 1.295。muMaxLeMin：若一切非零向量都不短于 r，则 μmax ≤ −ln r + ½ ln 7；所以 μmax = 1.8 时，任何大于 √7·e−1.8 ≈ 0.437 的 r 都做不到这一点，必有更短的非零向量。屋顶越平，两个半径越接近。
  6. 胶合把屋顶压平（示意模型）。同一个份额 e 把每个滤过点按比例压向平均斜率线：deg 变成 deg − e·(deg − 5/7·rk)。这是一个保持凸性的剪切，屋顶的形状不变，只是变平；e → 1 时 μmax → 5/7。e 与 g、b 的关系是示意，不是定理。

One mirror shows you once; link the theta lifts into a corridor and every extra mirror buys one more layer of "derivative". The question is how far the chain can go before its cost eats the gain. The page draws that account as two scenes on one rail: the chain budget b = m/√X. The page is a numerical illustration with an illustrative model, not a proof.

  1. The chain. On the left of the stage is U(X₀) → U(X₁) → … → U(Xₘ): each node is one theta lift, with √X = 5, so m = 5b. Amber marks the step in progress. With one character the chain is a single magenta line; once the gluing is orthogonal (g ≥ 0.6) it is three cyan lines glued together at every node.
  2. The seesaw. The end of the chain meets a violet rhombus: the lifted period on its left carries the m Hecke characters μ₁ … μₘ, and the seesaw trades it for the Bessel period on its right, where not one character is left. The big-cell coordinates of that integral are the Lean theorem bigCellMinors; undoing one "mirror" costs only the scalar |𝔽|, which is final_gaussOp, checked term by term on 𝔽₅ in the readouts.
  3. The 2/3 wall (illustrative model). The curves on the right are the zero-free edge σ as b grows. The magenta curve σ₁ = 2/3 + ⅓e−b/0.45 starts at σ = 1, is near 0.88 at b = 0.2 (m = 1), then hugs the 2/3 wall and never crosses it. The cyan curve σg = σ₁ − e/6 breaks through 2/3 once the gluing is orthogonal and sinks toward the green line σ = 1/2 (the Lean main theorem cited in chapter 1 puts every zero on that line; the curve here is a picture, not that proof). The shape of the curves is chosen for the picture and comes from no Lean theorem.
  4. The HN roof (exact convex geometry). A chain of saturated sublattices of a rank-7 lattice L gives the (rk, deg) points (0, 0), (1, h₁), (3, 4.4), (5, 5.4), (7, 5.0). The page computes their upper concave hull with a monotone chain: at h₁ = 1.8 the four slopes are 1.8 > 1.3 > 0.5 > −0.2 and the roof arches upward; drag h₁ below 4.4/3 ≈ 1.467 and the point (1, h₁) drops under the roof, so the hull jumps straight from (0, 0) to (3, 4.4). Amber marks the steepest, first rafter, whose slope is μmax; the grey dashed line is the mean slope μ(L) = 5/7 ≈ 0.714.
  5. The ridge forces a short vector (Lean). shortVector: L has a nonzero vector no longer than √rk·covol1/rk = √7·e−5/7 ≈ 1.295. muMaxLeMin: if every nonzero vector is at least r long, then μmax ≤ −ln r + ½ ln 7; so with μmax = 1.8 no r above √7·e−1.8 ≈ 0.437 can do that, and some nonzero vector is shorter. The flatter the roof, the closer the two radii.
  6. Gluing flattens the roof (illustrative model). The same share e pulls every filtration point toward the mean-slope line: deg becomes deg − e·(deg − 5/7·rk). This shear keeps convexity, so the roof keeps its shape and only gets flatter; as e → 1, μmax → 5/7. How e depends on g and b is a picture, not a theorem.
