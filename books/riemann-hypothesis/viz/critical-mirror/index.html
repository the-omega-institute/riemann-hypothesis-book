两个场景讲同一件事：零点只要离开临界线，就会在两处露馅。在复平面上，它会被函数方程映出一个孪生；在实数轴上，它会让素数的影子越过 √x 的堤坝。本页是数值演示，不是证明：页面只算有限个点，一般结论来自 Lean 定理。

  1. 镜子。函数方程把 ζ(s) 与 ζ(1 − s) 绑在一起，再加上 ζ(s) 是 ζ(s) 的共轭，零点 ρ = σ + it 就带出孪生 1 − ρ = (1 − σ) + it：高度不变，实部从 σ 变成 1 − σ。σ = 1 − σ 只有一个解 σ = 1/2，所以临界线是镜子里唯一不动的地方。拖动琥珀色的 ρ，品红的孪生总在绿线另一侧同样远的位置；把 ρ 放到绿线上，两者重合。
  2. 只需清空一半。左半带的零点都是右半带零点的镜像。Lean 定理 rh_of_right 正是这一步：只要 Re(s) > 1/2 处（除 s = 1 外）没有零点，黎曼猜想就成立。负偶数 −2、−4 是平凡零点，s = 1 是极点，它们都在临界带之外。
  3. 冒牌零点。非本原特征的 L 函数等于本原特征的 L 函数再乘几枚欧拉因子 1 − ψ(p)p−s。舞台上画的是 ψ(p) = 1 的情形：1 − p−s 在 s = 2πki/ln p 处为零，沿紫色虚线 Re(s) = 0 一级级排上去。只要 Re(s) > 0，就有 |p−s| = p−Re(s) < 1，因子不可能为零：这些冒牌货一个也进不了临界带（第一章的引理 Claim.factor_ne_zero）。
  4. 素数的影子。冯·曼戈尔特函数在素数幂 pk 处取 ln p，其余为 0。Sz(x) = Σn ≤ x Λ(n)(x/n − 1) 磨平了素数的锯齿，减去光滑主项 x ln x − (1 + γ)x 得到 Gz(x)。页面逐项算出它：Gz(10) ≈ 1.86080，Gz(100) ≈ 1.72169，都贴着 ln 2π ≈ 1.83788。青色曲线 Gz(x) − ln 2π 在 2 ≤ x ≤ 120 上一直待在绿色虚线 ±0.05√x 之间。
  5. 朗道陷阱。显式公式（文献结果）把 Gz(x) − ln 2π 写成每对零点贡献一项 −2 Re(xρ/(ρ(ρ − 1)))。这一项的振幅按 xσ 增长：σ = 1/2 时与 √x 同阶；σ = 0.78 时增长更快，终会越过任何 C√x，舞台上它在 x ≈ 66.89 冲出 0.05√x。反过来，Lean 定理 zetaLandau 说：只要 Gz 的一侧被 C·xθ 压住，Re(s) > θ 处就没有零点。品红波是假想的示意（MODEL），真实的 ζ 没有这样的零点。

Both scenes tell the same story: a zero that leaves the critical line gives itself away twice. In the complex plane the functional equation mirrors it into a twin; on the real line it pushes the shadow of the primes over the √x barrier. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof: it computes finitely many points, and the general statements come from the Lean theorems.

  1. The mirror. The functional equation ties ζ(s) to ζ(1 − s), and ζ(s) is the conjugate of ζ(s); so a zero ρ = σ + it brings along the twin 1 − ρ = (1 − σ) + it: same height, real part σ turned into 1 − σ. The equation σ = 1 − σ has the single solution σ = 1/2, so the critical line is the only place the mirror leaves fixed. Drag the amber ρ and the magenta twin stays as far away on the other side of the green line; put ρ on the green line and the two coincide.
  2. Clearing half is enough. Every zero in the left half of the strip is the image of one in the right half. The Lean theorem rh_of_right is exactly this step: if there is no zero with Re(s) > 1/2 (apart from s = 1), the Riemann Hypothesis holds. The negative even integers −2, −4 are the trivial zeros and s = 1 is the pole; all of them lie outside the critical strip.
  3. Impostor zeros. The L-function of an imprimitive character is the L-function of a primitive one times a few Euler factors 1 − ψ(p)p−s. The stage shows the case ψ(p) = 1: 1 − p−s vanishes at s = 2πki/ln p, rung after rung up the dashed violet line Re(s) = 0. As soon as Re(s) > 0 we have |p−s| = p−Re(s) < 1, so the factor cannot vanish: not one of these impostors gets into the critical strip (the lemma Claim.factor_ne_zero of chapter 1).
  4. The shadow of the primes. The von Mangoldt function is ln p at a prime power pk and 0 elsewhere. Sz(x) = Σn ≤ x Λ(n)(x/n − 1) smooths the saw-teeth of the primes, and subtracting the smooth main term x ln x − (1 + γ)x leaves Gz(x). The page computes it term by term: Gz(10) ≈ 1.86080 and Gz(100) ≈ 1.72169, both close to ln 2π ≈ 1.83788. The cyan curve Gz(x) − ln 2π stays between the dashed green lines ±0.05√x all along 2 ≤ x ≤ 120.
  5. The Landau trap. The explicit formula (a literature result) writes Gz(x) − ln 2π as one term −2 Re(xρ/(ρ(ρ − 1))) per pair of zeros. That term swings with an amplitude growing like xσ: of the order of √x when σ = 1/2; faster when σ = 0.78, so that it eventually beats any C√x; on the stage it breaks 0.05√x near x ≈ 66.89. Conversely, the Lean theorem zetaLandau says that once one side of Gz is held below C·xθ, there is no zero with Re(s) > θ. The magenta wave is a would-be sketch (MODEL); the real ζ has no such zero.
